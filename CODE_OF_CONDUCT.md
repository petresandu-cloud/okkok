# Code of conduct

This is a small project with a plain rule: treat people here the way the
report treats an app. Say what you found, say how you know, say what to do.
No guessing about motives, no contempt, no piling on.

Specifically:

- Findings, not insults. A wrong verdict is a bug report, not a verdict on
  the person who wrote the check.
- Evidence with claims. "It fails on my app" comes with the rule reference
  and the finding text; "you are wrong" comes with the rule page.
- No harassment of any kind, in issues, pull requests, discussions or in
  private about this project. No slurs, no threats, no sexual attention, no
  publishing of anyone's private details.
- Store staff and reviewers are people too. Nothing here is a place to
  attack Apple's or Google's reviewers by name.

Editerra AB moderates this repository. A first breach gets a warning and the
offending text edited or removed; a repeat, or anything severe, gets the
account blocked from the repository. Report a problem to
contact@editerra.se; it is read by a person and answered.
