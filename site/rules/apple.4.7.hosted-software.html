Blocks submissionApp Store

Mini apps, streaming games, chatbots, plug-ins or emulator games the app offers follow the hosting rules

The question The listing suggests the app offers software it does not embed. Does that software get privacy compliance, moderation and in-app purchase; is no native API exposed to it; is no data or permission shared without per-instance consent; is there an index with universal links; is it age-gated?

A person or a model answers it from the app's texts and screens; the answer is kept only while the facts it saw are unchanged.

Applies when listing:\b(mini ?apps?|mini ?games?|streaming games?|chatbot|plug-?ins?|emulator)

Checked from built, not yet uploaded, on a test track, submitted and in review, published

The rule, in our words

  • App Review Guidelines: Software the app offers but does not embed, such as HTML5/JavaScript mini apps and mini games, streaming games, chatbots, plug-ins and emulator-downloaded games, must follow the guidelines and the law; the app must apply the privacy rules to it, give it content filtering, reporting and user blocking, and route its digital goods through in-app purchase; it may not expose native platform APIs to that software without Apple's prior permission, may not share data or permissions with it without user consent each time, must publish an index with universal links to all of it, and must let users identify software above the app's age rating and gate it by verified or declared age.

Okkok never stores the stores' text. Each item above is our paraphrase, and the link is the page it rests on, verified unchanged by fingerprint on every run.

The other store

  • Google requires only an in-app report/flag path and Restricted Content prevention; Apple also requires content filtering, blocking, age identification, no native API exposure without permission, per-use consent for data sharing, and IAP routing. apple-stricter
